Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who uses this app

auto-shorts is used only by its operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What data it accesses

With the operator's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status on the operator's channel;
  • list the channel's own uploads and their privacy status;
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, retention, likes, shares, subscribers gained) for the videos it produced.

It does not access, collect or store personal data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only as a private secret in the operator's environment. Aggregated per-video statistics are stored in the operator's private project files and are used only to improve future videos. No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings, after which stored analytics are deleted on request.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel operator via the email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
